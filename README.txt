BAIT_UL_MAAL Website
=====================

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

Before publishing:
1. Replace the QR placeholder in index.html with your real QR image.
2. Replace UPI ID, phone, email and address.
3. Add your real gallery photos.
4. Replace the About text with your official organisation details.

To test: open index.html in a browser.
To publish: upload these files to a web host (for example GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or your hosting provider).
